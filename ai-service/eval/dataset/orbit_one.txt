Orbit One Smartwatch — Product Overview

The Orbit One is a smartwatch made by Lumen, released in March 2026. It is designed
for everyday fitness tracking and notifications while keeping a long battery life.

Display and Design
The Orbit One has a 1.4-inch AMOLED display with a resolution of 454 by 454 pixels.
The case is made of aircraft-grade aluminium and the watch weighs 32 grams without
the strap. It is available in three colours: midnight black, sage green, and rose gold.

Battery and Charging
In typical use the battery lasts 7 days on a single charge. In low-power mode, where
the always-on display is turned off, it lasts up to 14 days. Charging is done through a
magnetic charger that takes the watch from 0 to 100 percent in 90 minutes.

Health and Sensors
The Orbit One includes a heart rate sensor, an SpO2 (blood oxygen) sensor, built-in
GPS, and an accelerometer for step counting and sleep tracking. It does not include an
ECG sensor or a blood pressure sensor.

Water Resistance
The watch is rated 5 ATM, which means it can be worn while swimming in water up to
50 metres deep. It is not designed for scuba diving.

Compatibility
The Orbit One works with phones running Android 9 or newer, and iPhones running
iOS 14 or newer. The companion app is called Lumen Fit.

Price and Warranty
The Orbit One is priced at $199. It comes with a 2-year limited warranty that covers
manufacturing defects.
